Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our paperless bookkeeping services.

Paperless bookkeeping means all your financial records are managed digitally. You upload receipts, invoices, and bank statements via our secure portal or app, and our team categorises, reconciles, and maintains your books entirely in the cloud using HMRC-approved software like Xero, QuickBooks, or Sage.
Making Tax Digital is an HMRC initiative requiring businesses to maintain digital records and submit tax returns using compatible software. MTD for VAT is already mandatory for VAT-registered businesses. MTD for Income Tax Self-Assessment (ITSA) applies to self-employed businesses and landlords with annual income above £50,000 from April 2026, and above £30,000 from April 2027. We ensure full MTD compliance using HMRC-recognised software.
We support all major UK cloud accounting platforms including Xero, QuickBooks, Sage, Zoho Books, KashFlow, VT Software, and Capium. We can also work with your existing setup or recommend the best platform for your business needs.
